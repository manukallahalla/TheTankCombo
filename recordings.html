Recordings

These are practice recordings — rehearsals, run-throughs, the odd take that surprised us. Newest first.

The interesting part of a young band isn't any single performance. It's the distance between them. A tune turns up here stiff and over-thought, and a few weeks later it breathes. Most groups only put out the take they're happiest with. We'd rather leave the whole run standing, clams and all, and let the improvement do the talking.
