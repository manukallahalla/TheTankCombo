Neil

Bass Trombone

Neil plays bass trombone.

Neil has been playing jazz for over three years now, built on a five-year foundation in overall music experience. He got into jazz when he learned that he could play as loud as he wants, and this combo really helped shape his understanding of it. At the moment his favorite song is “Birdland” by Joe Zawinul.

Tank! has no upright or electric bass — Neil holds down the low end on bass trombone instead, trading the job back and forth with the piano's left hand.
