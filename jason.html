Jason

Alto Saxophone

Jason plays alto saxophone.

Jason has been playing for three years, mostly by ear and imitation — the players he keeps coming back to are Paul Desmond, Tomoaki Baba and Patrick Bartley. He's a cool jazz and bossa nova player at heart, and he plays both as much as he listens to them.

Jason's style leans soulful. Whereas Charlie Parker or Sonny Stitt play fast, burning bebop lines, he'd rather build a story one idea at a time, the way Paul Desmond does.
