About

We all play in the jazz band at Gunn High School in Palo Alto, but we wanted to take things further. Tank! is how we reach a wider audience, and it gives us chances to perform that a school schedule simply can't.

Our combo is built around a sax-and-trumpet front line that carries the melody and the harmony. Behind it, the rhythm section holds the groove: piano, drums and — a strange choice for a group this small — a bass trombone in place of an upright bass. We play a wide range of styles: swing, bossa nova and Latin, hard bop, and even spooky music or video game classics.

We've played one gig so far, at Greenmeadow here in Palo Alto, and we're ready for more. We'd love to play your next event.
