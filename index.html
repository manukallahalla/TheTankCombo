About

Tank! plays jazz the way it was meant to be heard — loud enough to fill the room, tight enough to turn on a dime, loose enough to surprise you.

Our front line pairs saxophone and trumpet over the growl of a bass trombone, an unusual voicing that gives the horns real weight. Underneath, piano and drums keep things swinging through standards, hard bop, soul-jazz and the occasional arrangement nobody asked for.

We play clubs, private parties, weddings, receptions and festivals — anywhere with room for five people and a little volume.
