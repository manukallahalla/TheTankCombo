About

We all go to Gunn High School in Palo Alto. We started it on our own and we run the whole thing ourselves.

Our front line is sax, trumpet and bass trombone. Bass trombone is a strange choice for a group this small, but we needed a bass player. It gives us a lot more low end than most quintets have. Piano and drums cover everything else. We mostly play standards, hard bop and soul-jazz.

We've played one gig so far, at Greenmeadow here in Palo Alto. We're after the next one. If you have room for five people and somewhere to plug in, we'll probably say yes.

We're students at Gunn, but Tank! is not a Gunn band.
